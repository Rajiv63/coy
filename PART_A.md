# Part A: Feature Suggestions for Coy

I signed up on app.getcoy.com with phone OTP, added crushes (3/3 free slots), verified my email from Settings (the code arrived by email), opened the invite link ("Invite 3 friends, get a slot free"), and looked at the extra-slots option.

## Feature 1: Confirm before saving a crush, and a status after adding

**What it is:** Right now, when I tap "Add crush", the crush is saved immediately, the slot is used, and the heads-up is sent. The screen itself says "Adding is final: you can't remove someone once they're on your list."

I would add a confirmation step before anything is saved. After I tap "Add crush", a small box would show the name and the full number I typed (for example "Rita, +91 70816 64465") and ask: "Is this correct? This will use 1 of your 3 slots." There would be two buttons: "Confirm" and "Edit". Nothing is saved, no slot is used and no heads-up is sent until I tap "Confirm". If I tap "Edit", I go back to the form and fix the name or number.

After adding, each crush in "Your crushes" would also show a small status, such as "Heads-up sent", or "Could not be delivered" if the number is not reachable. It would show nothing about whether the crush opened or reacted to it.

**Why it is required:** Adding is final and I only get 3 free slots. A single typo in the number would permanently use a slot and send the heads-up to the wrong person, and I would never know. Since adding must stay final so that nobody can test names, the right fix is to prevent mistakes before saving, not to allow removing afterwards. The confirmation costs one tap, and the status tells me the product worked. A user who loses a slot to a typo is also less likely to buy more.

**Privacy check:** Adding stays final once confirmed, so nobody can add and remove names to test who likes them. The status is about my own action only. It must never show whether the crush read, opened or reacted to the heads-up, and "Could not be delivered" should not reveal whether the number is on Coy.

## Feature 2: A next step after the free slots are used (swipe-style Discover)

**What it is:** When I reached 3/3, the home card only changed to "Get more slots". There was nothing else to do except pay or invite friends. I would add an optional Discover tab (the same kind of swipe feature I built in Part B), so the main Coy experience stays as it is. It would show people who have joined Coy from my contacts or college, so users have something to do after their slots are full. The rule stays the same: likes stay hidden until both people like each other.

**Why it is required:** Right now a user who is out of slots has no reason to open the app again, so retention drops and the only way back is a paywall. A Discover tab gives a reason to return. It also connects to the slot system: liking someone from Discover could use a slot, which makes extra slots (₹49, ₹129, ₹199 packs) more useful.

**Privacy check:** Nobody should see who liked them unless it is mutual, the same as the current model.

## Smaller observation

The email with the verification code is plain text with just the code and a short line. Adding Coy's branding, a larger code and the app colours would make it feel more trustworthy, since it comes from an app about privacy.
