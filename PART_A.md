# Part A: Feature Suggestions for Coy

I signed up on app.getcoy.com with phone OTP, added crushes (3/3 free slots), verified my email from Settings (code arrived by email), opened the invite link ("Invite 3 friends, get a slot free"), and looked at the extra-slots option.

## Feature 1: Heads-up status for the person who added the crush

**What it is:** After I add a crush, the app says "They get an anonymous heads-up right away", but I never see whether that actually happened. I would add a small status next to each crush in "Your crushes", such as "Heads-up sent", and "Not delivered, will retry" if it fails (for example a wrong number). It would show nothing about whether the crush opened or reacted to it.

I would also add a short correction window. If I notice I typed a wrong name or number, I could edit or remove that crush within a few minutes (for example 5 minutes), and the slot would be given back. After that window, adding stays final as it is today. The name is only visible to me, so changing it is always safe; changing the number can be limited to this one-time window.

**Why it is required:** Adding is final: a crush can't be removed and uses one of only 3 free slots. If I mistype a number, the slot is spent and the real person never hears anything. Without feedback I can't tell if the product worked, which makes users hesitate before adding and weakens trust. A wrong or failed delivery is a lost slot, and users who hit it are the least likely to buy more.

The Add a crush screen says adding is final and cannot be undone, so a single typo in the number permanently uses one of only 3 slots and sends the heads-up to the wrong person.

**Privacy check:** The status is about my own action only. It must never show whether the crush read, opened or reacted to the heads-up, because that would leak their feelings.

## Feature 2: A next step after the free slots are used (swipe-style Discover)

**What it is:** When I reached 3/3, the home card only changed to "Get more slots". There is nothing else to do except pay or invite friends. I would add a Discover tab (the same kind of swipe feature I built in Part B) so users have something to do after their slots are full, while keeping the rule that likes stay hidden until both people like each other.

**Why it is required:** Right now a user who is out of slots has no reason to open the app again, so retention drops and the only way back is a paywall. A Discover tab gives a reason to return, and it connects to the slot system: liking someone from Discover could use a slot, which makes extra slots more useful.

**Privacy check:** Nobody should see who liked them unless it is mutual, the same as the current model.
